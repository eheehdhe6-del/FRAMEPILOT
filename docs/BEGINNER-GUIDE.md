# FramePilot Beginner User Guide

Welcome to **FramePilot** — a playful, paper-first drawing and animation direction studio built so anyone can sketch characters, compose scenes, animate movement, and export structured instructions for AI coding agents like OpenCode.

## 1. Start a Project
1. Open FramePilot to see your **Project Library**.
2. Tap **New Project**, type a name, pick an aspect ratio (`9:16` for Reels/Shorts, `16:9` for YouTube, `1:1` Square, `4:5` Portrait, or Custom), and pick `12`, `24`, or `30 FPS`.
3. Tap **Start Creating** (or open the pre-loaded **Park Butterfly Reel** sample to explore!).

## 2. Three Connected Workspaces
- **Scene Layout**: Draw your characters, props, shapes, and text, and set up your background environment.
- **Draw Frames**: Add blank frames (`Blank +`), duplicate frames (`Duplicate`), adjust how long a drawing stays on screen (`Hold 2f`), turn on **Onion Skin** to trace the previous/next frame, and press **Play** to preview left-to-right.
- **Motion Studio**: Make objects move, bounce, orbit another object, follow a drawn path, morph shapes, emit particles, or zoom the camera without drawing every intermediate frame.

## 3. Essential Tools
- **Pencil / Pen / Brush**: Draw smoothly in document space (works zoomed in or out, with stylus pressure sensitivity).
- **Eraser**: Erase only the touched part of a stroke.
- **Fill**: Tap inside a shape, a closed loop, or the backdrop to fill it with your chosen color.
- **Shapes & Text**: Drag to place Lines, Rectangles, Ellipses, or Arrows; tap with **Text** to add kinetic titles.
- **Select & Lasso**: Tap or circle elements to move, scale, rotate, describe for AI, or animate.
- **Pinch & Pan**: Use two fingers anytime to zoom and pan around the paper; tap the Zoom `%` pill to fit the canvas back to screen.

## 4. Describe Your Art for AI (No Vision Needed)
Tap **AI Note** (or **Layers -> Describe for AI**) and answer simple questions:
- *What is this?*
- *What is it doing?*
- *How should it move?*
- *What should happen next?*

Any drawing you haven't labelled yet is honestly marked `unlabelled`—FramePilot never guesses.

## 5. Export for OpenCode
Tap **Export** in the top bar to inspect all generated files (`OPENCODE-PROMPT.md`, `START-HERE.md`, `sketches/spatial-map.md`, `project.json`, etc.), copy prompts to clipboard, or save/share a complete `.zip` package.
